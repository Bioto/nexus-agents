## 💰 COST-SAVING PRIORITIES

### High Impact Savings:
1. **Buy proteins in bulk** → Save ~$8-12 on meat
2. **Buy vegetables in bags** → Save ~$5-7 on produce
3. **Make taco seasoning** → Save ~$2-3
4. **Buy block cheese, shred yourself** → Save ~$1-2

### Medium Impact:
5. **Buy larger rice bag** (if you use it often) → Save ~$2-3
6. **Buy cabbage instead of coleslaw mix** → Save ~$1-2

### Estimated Total Savings: $19-28 per week

---

## 📋 SHOPPING STRATEGY

### Store Layout Order:
1. **Produce** (vegetables, fresh herbs)
2. **Meat/Seafood** (buy bulk, ask butcher to portion if needed)
3. **Dairy** (eggs, cheese)
4. **Pantry** (pasta, rice, canned goods)
5. **Condiments** (check what you have first!)

### Freezer Strategy:
- Portion and freeze extra meat immediately
- Label with date and weight
- Use frozen meat within 3 months for best quality

### Waste Prevention:
- ✅ Most items keep well (condiments, spices, frozen items)
- ⚠️ Use fresh produce within 5-7 days
- ⚠️ Use salmon the day you buy it
- ✅ Leftovers from 8-serving chicken recipe = lunch next day

---

## 📝 NOTES

- **Check your pantry first**: You may already have olive oil, cooking oil, black pepper, soy sauce, etc.
- **Sticky Ginger Soy Chicken serves 8**: Plan for 2 servings of leftovers (great for lunch!)
- **Consider meal prep**: Prep vegetables on Sunday to save time during the week
- **Flexibility**: If a protein is on sale, you can swap days in the meal plan
