### 1. Grilled Chicken with Broccoli and Rice
**Date:** Monday, December 1, 2025  
**Recipe ID:** 885e619b-822b-48cf-857f-3fb3bf8340fc

**Description:** A healthy and balanced dinner with grilled chicken breast, steamed broccoli, and white rice

**Servings:** 4  
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 30 minutes

#### Ingredients:
- 600g Chicken Breast
- 400g Broccoli
- 200g Rice (uncooked)
- 20ml Olive Oil
- 10g Garlic

#### Cooking Steps:
1. Season chicken breast with garlic, salt, and pepper
2. Grill chicken for 6-7 minutes per side until internal temperature reaches 165°F
3. Steam broccoli for 5-7 minutes until tender but still crisp
4. Cook rice according to package instructions
5. Serve chicken with broccoli and rice, drizzle with olive oil

**Description:** Hollowed zucchini boats filled with seasoned ground beef and topped with cheese

## 💾 Storage Tips & Best Practices

### General Storage Guidelines:
- **Use airtight containers** for all prepped ingredients to maintain freshness
- **Label containers with dates** to track shelf life
- **Store herbs in water** in the fridge for longer freshness
- **Freeze any meals or ingredients** that you won't use within the recommended time frame

### Specific Storage Recommendations:

#### Cooked Meals:
- Store in airtight containers in the fridge
- Generally keep for 3-4 days
- Reheat thoroughly before consuming
- Freeze portions you won't eat within 3-4 days

#### Fresh Produce:
- **Leafy greens (spinach)**: Use within 3-5 days
- **Broccoli, bell peppers, zucchini**: Store in breathable bags, use within 5-7 days
- **Mushrooms**: Store in paper bag in fridge, use within 3-5 days
- **Onions, garlic**: Store in cool, dark place, use within 1-2 weeks

#### Proteins:
- **Raw chicken**: Use within 1-2 days of purchase
- **Raw salmon**: Use within 1-2 days of purchase
- **Raw ground beef**: Use within 1-2 days of purchase
- **Cooked proteins**: Store in fridge, use within 3-4 days

#### Grains:
- **Cooked rice/quinoa**: Store in airtight container, use within 3-5 days
- **Cooked pasta**: Store in airtight container, use within 3-5 days

#### Pantry Items:
- **Spices (cumin, paprika)**: Store in cool, dark place, indefinite shelf life
- **Olive oil**: Store in cool, dark place, check expiration date
- **Canned black beans**: Store in fridge after opening, use within 3-5 days

## 📝 Notes on Shelf Life

### Critical Timing:
- **Fresh produce** should be used within their optimal shelf life for best taste and nutrition
- **Cooked meals** can generally be stored in the fridge for 3-4 days
- **Raw proteins** should be cooked within 1-2 days of purchase for safety
- **Always check for signs of spoilage** before use, especially with fresh produce

### Freezing Recommendations:
- Freeze any meals or ingredients that you won't use within the recommended time frame
- Portion meals before freezing for easier reheating
- Label frozen items with date and contents
- Use frozen items within 3 months for best quality

### Waste Prevention:
- Plan your prep schedule to minimize waste
- Use older ingredients first (first in, first out)
- Consider batch cooking and freezing portions
- Repurpose leftovers creatively
